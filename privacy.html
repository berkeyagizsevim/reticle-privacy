Reticle Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026
ReticleSupportPrivacy

Summary

Reticle is a macOS menu bar app that shows your latest screenshots so you can drag or paste them anywhere. Reticle does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. Everything happens on your Mac.

Data collected

None. Reticle has no accounts, analytics, advertising identifiers or tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

Your files

Reticle reads image files only from the folder you choose through the standard macOS open panel (a security-scoped, read-only permission). Files never leave your Mac. When you paste, the image is placed on your clipboard and the previous clipboard contents are restored afterwards.

Local storage

A few settings are kept on your Mac with Apple's standard UserDefaults: the chosen folder, the trial start date and your purchase status. They are removed when you delete the app.

In-App Purchase (Pro)

Browsing, drag and drop and the automatic popup are free. The paste actions are unlocked by a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple's App Store (StoreKit). Your payment details go to Apple, never to Reticle. Your device contacts Apple's servers for this; it is part of the App Store purchase system, not a connection Reticle opens itself.

Network access

Apart from the App Store purchase flow described above, Reticle makes no network connections.

Permissions

Reticle runs in the Apple App Sandbox and asks only for what it needs:

  • Read-only access to the folder you choose.
  • Accessibility, only if you grant it, solely to send the paste keystroke (Cmd+V) to the app you select. Reticle does not read other apps' content.

It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos.

Children's privacy

Reticle does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it collects no data at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be published here and the date above will be refreshed.
