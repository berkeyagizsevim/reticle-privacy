Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to support@sparkup.studio. In the app you can also right-click the menu bar icon and choose Give Feedback… or Report a Bug….

Getting started

  1. Click the Reticle icon in the menu bar and choose the folder where macOS saves your screenshots (usually the Desktop).
  2. Take a screenshot (Cmd+Shift+4). It appears in the tray, and the tray opens by itself.
  3. Drag it into any app, or select it and use Paste to Selected App (Pro).

Deleting screenshots

Select one or more screenshots, click Move to Trash in the Screenshots header and confirm. The files go to the Trash, they are never deleted permanently, and Reticle never deletes anything on its own.

If Reticle says it needs permission to move files, click Choose Folder… and pick your screenshots folder again. A locked file stays where it is and Reticle tells you so.

Granting Accessibility access

The paste buttons send a Cmd+V keystroke to the app you chose, which needs the macOS Accessibility permission. You grant it once:

  1. Click Grant Access in the yellow banner at the top of the Reticle popover. macOS shows its standard permission prompt; choose Open System Settings.
  2. Under Privacy & Security > Accessibility, switch Reticle on. If Reticle is not listed, click + and choose Reticle from Applications.
  3. Click Relaunch in the green banner at the top of the popover.

Drag and drop works without this permission.

Restoring a purchase

Right-click the menu bar icon and choose Restore Purchases (it is also on the locked card in the popover). Purchases are tied to your Apple ID.

Free trial

Pro includes a 14-day free trial. After that, a one-time purchase unlocks the paste actions. Browsing, drag and drop and the automatic popup stay free.

Known limitations

  • Reticle pastes into the last active window of the app you choose; macOS does not let a sandboxed app pick a specific window of another app.
  • Reticle reads images only from the folder you choose (PNG, JPG, HEIC, TIFF). To change it, right-click the menu bar icon > Change Screenshots Folder…

Privacy

Reticle collects no data. See the privacy policy.
